AI Clipper

A personal tool that turns long podcasts into short vertical clips and schedules them on its owner's own YouTube channel.

AI Clipper uses YouTube API Services to upload and schedule videos. Read our Privacy Policy.

What it does

  1. Takes a podcast video that the channel owner has the right to use.
  2. Uses AI to find the most interesting moments, then cuts them into short vertical clips with captions.
  3. Uploads the clips to the owner's YouTube channel and schedules them to publish at set times, using the YouTube Data API.

Who it is for

AI Clipper is used only by its owner, to manage their own YouTube channel. It is not offered to the public, and it does not access anyone else's account.

YouTube API Services

AI Clipper uses YouTube API Services. By using it, you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. See also the Google Privacy Policy and our Privacy Policy.
