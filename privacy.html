Privacy Policy

AI Clipper · Last updated: 5 October 2026

AI Clipper is a personal tool used only by its owner to create short clips and schedule them on the owner's own YouTube channel. This policy explains what information it uses and how.

YouTube API Services

AI Clipper uses YouTube API Services. By using AI Clipper you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of your information is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Information we access

  • Google account authorization: when the owner connects their YouTube channel, Google gives AI Clipper an access token that lets it upload videos to that channel.
  • Upload results: the ID and link of each video AI Clipper uploads, so it can keep a log of what has been scheduled.

AI Clipper does not read your subscribers, comments, messages, watch history, analytics or any other data from your Google or YouTube account.

How we use it

The information above is used only to upload clips to the owner's channel, set their title, description and publish time, and keep a record of what was uploaded. It is not used for advertising, profiling or any other purpose.

Where it is stored

AI Clipper runs on the owner's own computer. The access token and the upload log are stored only on that computer. They are not sent to any server operated by AI Clipper, because there is none.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share data obtained through Google or YouTube APIs with anyone. To find good moments in a podcast, the podcast's audio and transcript are sent to an AI service (Groq). No Google or YouTube account data is included.

Google API Services User Data Policy

AI Clipper's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Cookies

AI Clipper and this website do not use cookies or tracking.

Removing access and deleting data

You can remove AI Clipper's access to your Google account at any time at Google security settings → Third-party access. You can also ask us to delete any stored data by emailing the address below. Data is deleted within 30 days of the request, or immediately when access is removed and the tool is uninstalled.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
